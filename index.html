Methodology. FBT fit score (0–100) blends operational pain 35% (late-dispatch, cancellations, delivery days), scale 30% (log order volume) and margin headroom 20%, with a hard eligibility gate at 30 kg / 31.5 L per parcel and exclusion of sellers already on FBT. Cost/benefit assumes ~28% lower per-order cost on FBT and a pain-scaled volume uplift capped at +30%, benchmarked to TikTok's UK FBT pilot figures. All sellers, brands and numbers are invented.
